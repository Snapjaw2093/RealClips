How it works

From one long upload to three ready clips

No editing software. No scrubbing through footage.

  1. 01

    Start with one video

    A full listing walkthrough or a market update, however long it runs.

  2. 02

    We find the best moments

    RealClips reads what was said and picks three moments. Each opens with a strong hook and ends at a natural stopping point.

  3. 03

    Get three finished clips

    Cropped to vertical, captioned, and saved with a title and a note on why each moment was chosen.

What you get

Built for Shorts, not for editors

Every clip is ready to post the moment it lands.

Hooks, not random cuts

Slicing a video every 45 seconds gives you boring clips. RealClips chooses moments that open with a reason to keep watching and close on a finished thought, so each one works on its own for someone who has never seen the rest.

Hook first

Captions built in

Large, bold, word-timed captions sit in the lower-middle of the frame, so clips still land with the sound off.

Shorts-ready

Vertical 9:16 MP4s at 1080×1920.

Title and reason

Each clip comes with a title and a one-line reason it was picked.

The idea

The best 45 seconds of your listing video shouldn't be buried inside a twelve-minute walkthrough.
