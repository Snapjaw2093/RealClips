How it works

  1. 1

    Start with one video

    A full listing walkthrough or a market update, however long it runs.

  2. 2

    We find the best moments

    RealClips reads what was said and picks three 30 to 60 second moments. Each one opens with a strong hook and ends at a natural stopping point.

  3. 3

    Get three finished clips

    Vertical 1080x1920 video with large, easy-to-read captions already added, plus a title and a note on why each moment was chosen.

What you get

Shorts-ready format

Vertical 9:16 MP4s sized for YouTube Shorts.

Captions built in

Big, bold, word-timed captions, so clips work with the sound off.

Hooks, not random cuts

Moments are chosen for a strong opening and a clean finish, not just slicing at fixed times.
